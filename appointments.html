Know what to expect before your visit

Brilliant Healthcare brings dialysis care and diagnostic services together in one coordinated center. Our appointment system shows physician availability before you submit your request.

Please bring a valid ID, your physician’s referral or previous results when applicable, and a current list of medicines. The clinic will provide service-specific preparation instructions when your appointment is confirmed.

Our Services

Comprehensive Medical Services

From routine check-ups to specialized dialysis care, we offer a full range of medical services under one roof.

Hemodialysis

Advanced hemodialysis treatments using state-of-the-art machines to effectively filter waste and toxins from your blood.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Flexible peritoneal dialysis options with comprehensive training and support for home-based treatment.

Lab Diagnostics

Full-service diagnostic laboratory offering accurate blood tests, urine analysis, and specialized pathology services.

Cardiac Diagnostics

Comprehensive cardiac testing including ECG, echocardiograms, and stress tests for heart health assessment.

Radiology & Imaging

Digital X-ray, ultrasound, and other imaging services to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Health Checkups

Comprehensive health screening packages designed to detect health issues early and promote wellness.

    Before your appointment

    Use this guide to prepare for a smooth clinic visit. Contact our team if you need help at any stage.

    Before booking

    Choose the appropriate service and physician

    Wait for confirmation from the clinic

    Your selected time is reserved while an administrator reviews the request. Keep your booking reference for follow-up.
